## 🎥 Level 9 Conversational Sentences Practice Studio

For complex sentence sequences, SignSafe features a dedicated **Webcam Practice Studio** with word-by-word syntax analysis:

### 5 Structured Sentence Categories:
1. **Introductions & Identity (4 phrases):**
   * *Hello, Nice to Meet You* — `[HELLO 👋] + [MEET 🤝] + [HAPPY 😊]`
   * *My Name Is Priya* — `[MY / ME 🙋] + [NAME 📛] + [PRIYA 👧]`
   * *I Am Deaf* — `[ME 🙋] + [DEAF 🧏]`
   * *I Know Sign Language* — `[ME 🙋] + [KNOW 💡] + [SIGN LANGUAGE 🤟]`
2. **Daily Questions & Inquiries (4 phrases):**
   * *What Is Your Name?* — `[YOUR 👉] + [NAME 📛] + [WHAT ❓]`
   * *Where Are You From?* — `[YOU 👉] + [LOCATION 🏡] + [WHERE 📍]`
   * *What Do You Do? (Work)* — `[YOU 👉] + [WORK 💼] + [WHAT ❓]`
   * *What Is Your Father's Name?* — `[YOUR 👉] + [FATHER 👨] + [NAME 📛] + [WHAT ❓]`
3. **Roles & Warm Wishes (2 phrases):**
   * *My Profession (Student / Doctor)* — `[MY 🙋] + [ROLE 🎓]`
   * *Hope You Are Healthy & Happy* — `[FRIENDS 👥] + [HEALTHY 💪] + [HAPPY 😊]`
4. **Signing Speed & Assistance (3 phrases):**
   * *Please Sign Slowly* — `[PLEASE 🙏] + [SIGN 🤟] + [SLOW 🐢]`
   * *Could You Please Sign Again?* — `[PLEASE 🙏] + [SIGN 🤟] + [REPEAT 🔁]`
   * *You Are Signing Very Fast* — `[YOU 👉] + [SIGNING 🤟] + [VERY FAST ⚡]`
5. **Comprehension & Understanding (2 phrases):**
   * *Yes, I Understand* — `[YES 👍] + [I 🙋] + [UNDERSTAND 💡]`
   * *I Don't Understand* — `[NO 👎] + [I 🙋] + [DONT UNDERSTAND 🤷]`

### Studio Capabilities:
* **Video Playback Speed Controller:** Toggle between `0.5x (Slow Motion)`, `0.75x (Rehearsal)`, and `1.0x (Standard Speed)` to study complex transitions.
* **ISL Sign Structure Gloss Badges:** Visual word-by-word sequential chips illustrating grammatical structure.
* **Self-Rehearsal Camera Mirror:** Real-time webcam reflection alongside the instructor video.
* **Progress Verification:** "Mark as Practiced ✓" button logs completions with celebratory audio feedback.

## 🏆 Assessment Test Arena & Printable Certificates

* **Dual Examination Formats:**
  1. **AI Camera Practical Exam:** Real-time computer vision verification of student sign accuracy.
  2. **Visual Theory MCQ Exam:** 4-option visual multiple-choice quiz with embedded video prompts.
* **Customizable Timers:** 15s, 20s, 30s per question or untimed mode.
* **Accredited Printable Certificate:** Automated calculation of Distinction / First Class grades (`A+`, `A`, `B`) and one-click printable official ISL Certificate.

---

## 🚨 SafeSOS Universal Emergency Suite

* **Visual Strobe Notification:** High-contrast flashing strobe alert designed specifically for deaf individuals who cannot hear standard sirens.
* **1-Tap WhatsApp & Cellular GSM SMS (`sms:+91...`):** Dispatches distress alerts even without mobile data during power cuts or disasters.
* **Live GPS Geo-Tracking:** Instant latitude/longitude dispatch to registered guardians with Google Maps coordinates.
* **Dynamic Safety Status Dispatch:** One-tap confirmation: "I AM SAFE NOW", "I NEED ASSISTANCE", "I AM IN DANGER".
